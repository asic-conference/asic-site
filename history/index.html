Annual Summer Interdisciplinary Conference

ASIC — A History of Meetings

Since 2002, ASIC has gathered cognitive scientists and kindred researchers each summer in an outstanding adventure setting — from the granite of Squamish to the Dolomites, the Pyrenees, and the Alps. Browse any year below.

Each meeting runs for six days, with talks in the late afternoon and evening and days left free for the mountains. Select a year to see its full information.
