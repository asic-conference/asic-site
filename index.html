27th Annual Summer Interdisciplinary Conference

ASIC 2027

Santa Caterina di Valfurva, near Bormio, Italy August 2–8, 2027 · six days of talks (Aug 2–7) Hotel Residence 3-Signori

The 27th Annual Summer Interdisciplinary Conference (ASIC 2027) will be held at Hotel Residence 3-Signori in Santa Caterina di Valfurva, a twenty-minute drive from Bormio, Italy — home of the legendary Stelvio Pass.

ASIC gathers cognitive scientists and kindred researchers each summer for a week of interdisciplinary talks, with days left free for the mountains. Talks run every afternoon and evening across six consecutive days, August 2–7, 2027, in a single daily session intentionally organized to maximize the variety of each day's presentations. The conference is open to all interested parties and their families — no invitation is needed to attend.
